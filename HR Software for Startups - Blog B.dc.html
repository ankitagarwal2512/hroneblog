The short answer

The 10 best HR software for startups scaling 10 to 200 employees in 2026 are HROne, Keka, greytHR, Zoho People, Zimyo, factoHR, Kredily, BambooHR, Deel and Rippling. HROne ranks first for India-first startups crossing 100 employees, running 127 pre-built workflows from pre-boarding to exit clearance, with a 4.8/5 G2 rating across 2,074 verified reviews.

10 vendors assessed 8 decision-grade criteria ₹ costs shown pre- and post-GST 7 checks to run in the demo

Picking HR software while you are growing from 10 to 200 people is a high-stakes call, because the system you install at 25 employees quietly decides how your payroll, attendance and compliance behave at 200. For this guide, 10 vendors serving Indian startups and mid-market teams were evaluated against defined operational, compliance and cost criteria rather than popularity or badge count.

The primary reader here is the person inside the tool every day: the first HR hire, the HR ops lead, or the founder still doing payroll on a Sunday night. The shadow readers are the CHRO, the CFO signing the invoice, and the IT lead who owns access and data.

One honest disclosure before the table

Most ranking pages on this query are published by vendors who place themselves first without saying so. HROne publishes this one, and its own stated sweet spot is 100 to 5,000 employees — founder Karan Jain says startup-tier HR software is not the segment the company chases.

So read the ranking as banded: HROne earns the top slot for the 100-to-200 half of your journey, and the sub-₹100 tools below genuinely win the 10-to-50 half.

Method

How these 10 were evaluated

Eight decision-grade measures, not feature checklists.

India statutory depth

EPF ECR generation, ESIC wage-ceiling logic, multi-state PT and LWF slabs, TDS with Form 16 release.

Scale headroom (10→200)

Multi-entity support, layered access rights, workflow count, cost-centre depth before re-implementation.

Time to value

Published go-live timeline, migration effort, and whether billing starts at purchase or at go-live.

Pricing transparency

Published INR rates, minimum billing blocks, and the all-in number at 200 and 500 employees.

Adoption reality

Mobile coverage, clicks to close a routine task, self-service depth for non-desk staff.

Support model

Dedicated SPOC versus email-only queues, and publicly reported NPS or CSAT.

Verified review evidence

Volume, recency and the pattern of complaints — not just the average score.

Exit cost

Data export rights, retention windows and lock-in length.

Keka is an India-headquartered cloud HRMS founded in 2014, serving over 12,500 customers with native payroll for India, the US and the UAE. Core HR centralises records, documents, org charts and onboarding. India payroll automates multi-state statutory computation and payslip release. Keka Hiring is quote-based, and Keka Learn is an add-on at ₹60 per employee per month with a 50-employee minimum.

Zimyo positions on module breadth at a low published entry price, covering core HR, payroll, performance and engagement. Independent pricing analysis puts the all-in Indian buyer cost near ₹94 per employee monthly after 18% GST, which is a useful reality check on the ₹50 sticker.

The GST lesson every buyer forgets

Teams compare a pre-GST quote from one vendor against a post-GST quote from another, and the cheaper one looks 18% cheaper than it is. Ask every vendor for the post-GST number in writing before you compare anything.

The rest of the field

06–10 · The specialists

Each of these wins a narrow job well. None of them is a general-purpose answer for an India-only team scaling to 200.

06

factoHR

Attendance-plus-payroll for device-heavy SMBs

Pairs biometric hardware integration with statutory payroll, which fits factory and multi-shift teams whose punch data lives on machines. G2 lists five editions from ₹2,999 to ₹5,999 per month, but the employee-block basis is not published — insist on a written all-in quote at your projected headcount.

07

Kredily

Free-forever HRMS for sub-25 teams

A genuinely free core plan covering employee database, onboarding, payroll, payout and self-service, with automated PF and ESI plus Form 16 and 24Q. Hard limits apply: a 250MB database cap and deactivation after three months of inactivity. Geo-fencing and biometric sync are paid-tier only.

Where free stops being free: use it to get compliant fast under 25 people. Do not build three years of employee history inside a 250MB cap you will hit.

08

BambooHR

Clean people database for US-centric teams

Well-liked for records, onboarding and time off. It has no India statutory payroll — no PF, ESI, PT or TDS — and multiple verified reviewers describe it straining past 50 employees. Fine for a US-registered entity, wrong for an Indian one.

09

Deel

Global EOR and contractor compliance

The default brand in cross-border hiring: EOR at $599 per employee per month, contractors at $49. Excellent at the job it does.

Do not buy Deel for an India-only team. One EOR employee at $599 a month costs roughly ₹50,000 — more than most Indian HRMS platforms charge for 200 people.

10

Rippling

Unified HR and IT provisioning

Issues software access and laptops from the same record that holds employment data, which suits distributed tech teams tired of onboarding someone twice. India statutory payroll is not its native strength and India pricing is not published.

Demo checklist

Seven things to make them show you live

Not slides. Ask for these on screen, in a sandbox with your own data, in one demo.

  1. 1Generate a live EPF ECR file from a sample month and open it.
  2. 2Cross an employee over the ESIC wage ceiling mid-cycle and show what the system does.
  3. 3Run PT and LWF for two states in the same payroll run.
  4. 4Release a Form 16 to an employee's own document section, end to end.
  5. 5Back-date a CTC revision and show the arrear-day calculation it produces.
  6. 6Add a second legal entity live, and state the cap on entity count in writing.
  7. 7Export everything and leave. Ask what you get, in what format, and how long they retain it.

Questions

Frequently asked

At what headcount should a startup move off spreadsheets?

Usually between 20 and 30 people, or the first month you run payroll across two states. Below that, a free tier handles it. Above it, the reconciliation time costs more than the subscription.

Will we have to re-implement when we cross 200?

Only if the system caps entities, workflow branching or access layers. Ask for those three limits in writing before you sign — they are the ones that force a migration, not employee count.

Is a free HRMS safe for payroll?

For statutory basics under 25 people, yes. Read the storage cap and inactivity policy first — a free account that deactivates after three months of quiet is not an archive of your payroll history.

How long does go-live actually take?

Thirty days is realistic for a core four-module setup — Core HR, Workforce, Time Office and Payroll — when your employee master is clean. Dirty master data, not software, is what stretches timelines.
